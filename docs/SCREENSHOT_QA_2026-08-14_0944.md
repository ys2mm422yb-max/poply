# Real iPhone QA — 2026-08-14 09:44

Source: three real iPhone Safari screenshots supplied by the owner after PR #28 / `c6841c2`.

## Verdict
Materially better than the pre-rebuild UI, but Milestone B remains OPEN. The app now has a coherent AAA foundation, yet the screenshots still read as a polished prototype rather than a premium commercial casual game.

## Board
Observed:
- the three horizontal job tickets are too information-dense for phone width;
- order titles and secondary data visibly clip or collide;
- three equal cards compete with the board instead of acting as lightweight purpose cues;
- the 7x7 grid is still visually dominated by empty cells when only 8 items exist;
- text/emoji-like navigation and HUD glyphs weaken the authored art language.

Required response:
- board job strip shows customer + required item icons + reward/status only; full order detail belongs in Orders;
- tapping a board customer opens that exact order;
- empty cells visually recede while retaining clear drop targets;
- use authored vector UI icons for HUD/navigation.

## Orders
Observed:
- quest cards are cleaner than the old white forms but still behave like three large stacked information cards;
- the screen has excess dead space and no single focal customer/action;
- all three orders have equal visual weight, so the player scans a dashboard instead of serving a guest.

Required response:
- show all three customers as a compact queue/selector;
- show exactly one selected order as the main service task;
- selected order owns the large requirement art, rewards, missing count and delivery CTA;
- board-to-orders navigation may preselect the tapped customer.

## Place
Observed:
- authored coastal scene and journey are a clear improvement;
- scene is still flatter and more diagrammatic than the target quality bar;
- next iterations should increase atmosphere, lighting, material depth and restoration payoff rather than adding more status UI.

## Acceptance gate for next visible release
- no clipped job title/data on the Board screenshot;
- Board purpose cues are visually subordinate to playable items;
- Orders has one obvious focal job rather than three equal giant cards;
- board-to-order customer selection is functional, not decorative;
- no emoji/text glyphs are used as primary HUD/navigation icons;
- Safari gets cache-busted CSS/JS URLs;
- Place remains authored and no legacy V2 live layer returns.
