# Real iPhone QA – 2026-08-14

Source: owner screenshots from the canonical Poply Pages build.

## Board
Observed:
- functionally coherent one-screen layout;
- too much empty visual mass in the 7x7 field;
- mini orders still read as web cards;
- hierarchy between current mission, active jobs and board is not authored strongly enough.

Required response:
- integrate jobs visually with the board;
- keep empty cells recessive;
- keep items/generators dominant;
- do not add extra navigation or scrolling.

## Orders
Observed:
- readable and functional;
- still form/card-like rather than a game-native quest screen;
- weak character/reward emphasis;
- large unused lower area.

Required response:
- quest hierarchy with character, needs, reward and action;
- fill the available game surface intentionally without giant empty cards;
- make the restoration purpose explicit without enterprise-dashboard styling.

## Place
Observed:
- structural progress over V2;
- current scene reads as flat concept/vector diagram, not a living coastal café;
- 2x3 restoration cards feel like status UI rather than world progression;
- insufficient atmosphere, lighting, depth and emotional payoff.

Required response:
- authored multi-layer scene with coast, building, light, depth and foreground;
- six upgrades become actual visible scene additions;
- replace tile wall with current-goal + journey progression;
- scene is the hero, UI supports it.

## Acceptance rule
Milestone B stays OPEN until real iPhone screenshots are materially closer to polished commercial casual-game presentation than these screenshots. Technical existence of six stages is not enough.
