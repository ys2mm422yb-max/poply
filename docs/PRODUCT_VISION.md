# Poply – Product Vision

## Vision reset
As of 2026-08-13, Poply is being redesigned from a short-session connect-and-pop puzzle into an original persistent merge-and-build casual game.

The reference is the broad structure of successful merge/meta games: persistent board, generators, item evolution, orders, rewards, restoration and new-place progression. Poply must keep its own world, art, characters, item chains and UI.

## Core player promise
Every merge should move the player toward something visible.

A player should be able to open Poply, create items, merge two identical items into a more valuable one, finish an order and immediately see how that helps improve the current Poply place.

## Main loop
1. Generate base items.
2. Merge identical same-tier items.
3. Discover higher item tiers and new families.
4. Fulfil several visible orders.
5. Earn coins/reputation/restoration progress.
6. Improve the current place.
7. Unlock new generators, chains, characters and places.
8. Return later to the same persistent board and world.

## Poply Places
The working meta concept is a journey through original Poply places rather than one restaurant forever.

The first vertical slice may use a cozy café/food-place theme because it makes generators and customer orders intuitive. Later places can introduce other themes and item families while keeping the same merge foundation.

This gives Poply long-term variety without reproducing one specific restaurant game.

## Board philosophy
- Persistent rather than level-resetting.
- Limited space creates interesting choices.
- Two identical items merge into exactly one next-tier item.
- Clear item families with satisfying discovery.
- Generators are long-term anchors.
- Storage and cleanup tools protect players from frustrating dead ends.
- Board state must save reliably.

## Order philosophy
- Multiple simultaneous orders.
- Early orders teach item chains.
- Later orders mix families and higher tiers.
- Rewards feel proportional to effort.
- Order delivery is immediate and visually satisfying.

## Progression philosophy
- Orders fund restoration/building.
- Restoration visibly changes the current scene.
- Milestones unlock new content.
- New content should create fresh board decisions, not just larger numbers.

## Feel
- Relaxing, colorful and tactile.
- Broad appeal without being babyish or strongly gender-coded.
- Clean enough to understand quickly, rich enough to reward long-term discovery.
- Strong merge snap, item upgrade reveal, reward collection and restoration payoff.
- Short satisfying actions inside a persistent long-term game.

## Engagement philosophy
Desired: discovery, collections, item chains, visible progress, short achievable goals, near-term restoration milestones and fair daily/event content later.

Not desired: hidden loss manipulation, intentionally bad drops before monetization, fake scarcity/countdowns, forced pay-to-win progression or aggressive energy gating before the core loop is proven fun.

## Content model
Poply should be designed as a content system rather than hard-coded one-off levels.

Data should define item families/tiers, generator outputs/upgrades, order requirements/rewards, place restoration steps, unlock conditions and chapter/place configuration.

This allows the game to grow without rewriting the core engine for every new place.

## Early success criteria
The new prototype succeeds when a first-time player can, without external explanation:
- move an item,
- merge two identical items,
- discover the next tier,
- understand a visible order,
- complete that order,
- collect a reward,
- improve the current place,
- and know what they want to work toward next.
